\section{CAN Transceiver Module --- WCMCU-230 (SN65HVD230)}

The standard CAN transceiver module used by RT, SYS, and PWT. Contains a TI SN65HVD230 3.3\,V CAN transceiver.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{Label on Module} & \textbf{Connects To} & \textbf{Notes} \\
\midrule
VCC / 3V3         & ESP32 3.3\,V pin     & \textbf{Must be 3.3\,V, not 5\,V} \\
GND               & ESP32 GND pin        & Common ground \\
CTX / TX / TXD / D & ESP32 TWAI TX GPIO   & From ESP32 to transceiver \\
CRX / RX / RXD / R & ESP32 TWAI RX GPIO   & From transceiver to ESP32 \\
CAN\_H             & Bus CAN\_H (screw term) & Differential high \\
CAN\_L             & Bus CAN\_L (screw term) & Differential low \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{Key facts:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
  \item 4 wires needed: VCC, GND, CTX ($\rightarrow$ TX), CRX ($\rightarrow$ RX)
  \item RS pin: pulled to GND by onboard 10\,k\textOmega\ --- locked in high-speed mode
  \item 120\,\textOmega\ terminator: onboard resistor controlled by solder jumper or DIP switch
  \item Current draw: $\sim$30\,mA at 3.3\,V
  \item Counterfeit warning: some modules receive but cannot transmit. Swap to isolate.
\end{itemize}

\section{CAN Controller Module --- MCP2515 SPI (MCP2515 + TJA1050)}

Used by RT for the high bus. Standalone SPI-to-CAN controller.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{Label} & \textbf{Connects To} & \textbf{Notes} \\
\midrule
VCC   & \textbf{5\,V} from ESP32 dev board & TJA1050 transceiver needs 5\,V \\
GND   & ESP32 GND            & Common ground \\
SCK   & ESP32 \gpio{36}      & SPI clock, $\leq$10\,MHz \\
SI (MOSI) & ESP32 \gpio{37}  & SPI data: MCU $\rightarrow$ MCP2515 \\
SO (MISO) & ESP32 \gpio{38}  & SPI data: MCP2515 $\rightarrow$ MCU \\
CS    & ESP32 \gpio{39}      & Chip select, active low \\
INT   & ESP32 \gpio{40}      & Interrupt: RX buffer ready, active low \\
CAN\_H & Bus CAN\_H (screw)   & High bus backbone \\
CAN\_L & Bus CAN\_L (screw)   & High bus backbone \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{Key facts:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
  \item SPI Mode 0 or 1; clock 8\,MHz safe, 10\,MHz max
  \item Crystal: 8\,MHz or 16\,MHz --- check laser etching; BRP must match
  \item 5\,V power required --- TJA1050 produces marginal CAN levels at 3.3\,V
  \item No onboard termination --- rely on CANalyst-II Ch1 or external 120\,\textOmega
\end{itemize}

\chapter{External Watchdog --- TPS3850}

Each ESP32 has a TPS3850 (or equivalent) window watchdog IC. If firmware stops toggling the WDI pin, the IC asserts a hardware reset within $\sim$100\,ms.

\section{Watchdog Wiring}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lccccl}
\toprule
\textbf{Controller} & \textbf{WDI GPIO} & \textbf{Toggle Rate} & \textbf{Timeout} & \textbf{TPS3850 MR} $\rightarrow$ \\
\midrule
RT ESP32-S3  & 21 & 100\,Hz (control\_task) & $\sim$100\,ms & ESP32 EN pin \\
SYS ESP32-S3 & 23 & 20\,Hz (safety\_task)  & $\sim$100\,ms & ESP32 EN pin \\
PWT ESP32-S3 & 21 & 20\,Hz                 & $\sim$100\,ms & ESP32 EN pin \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Wiring per controller:}
\begin{lstlisting}
GPIO (WDI pin) -> TPS3850 WDI
TPS3850 MR     -> ESP32 EN pin (active-low reset)
TPS3850 VCC    -> 3.3V
TPS3850 GND    -> GND
\end{lstlisting}

\section{Behavior on Reset}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
  \item GPIO toggles at specified rate in safety/control task
  \item If toggling stops $>$100\,ms: TPS3850 pulls MR LOW $\rightarrow$ ESP32 hardware reset
  \item All GPIOs go high-impedance $\rightarrow$ motor controller sees 0\,V throttle, all gear relays open
  \item Post-reset: firmware boots clean, starts in MANUAL mode, re-runs LBS sequences
  \item Startup delay $\sim$200\,ms before first toggle arms the watchdog
\end{enumerate}

\chapter{Gear Isolation \& Throttle DAC Circuits}

\section{TLP281 Optoisolator --- 72\,V Gear Sense}

The 72\,V gear selector signals must be galvanically isolated from the 3.3\,V MCU GPIOs. A TLP281-4 quad optoisolator provides this isolation.

\subsection{Channel Mapping}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{clll}
\toprule
\textbf{Channel} & \textbf{72\,V Gear Line} & \textbf{GPIO (SYS)} & \textbf{GPIO (MTR)} \\
\midrule
1 & Gear D & 12 & PB0 (encoded 16) \\
2 & Gear S & 13 & PB1 (encoded 17) \\
3 & Gear R & 14 & PB2 (encoded 18) \\
4 & (spare) & --- & --- \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Behavior:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
  \item 72\,V present $\rightarrow$ LED on $\rightarrow$ phototransistor conducts $\rightarrow$ GPIO reads LOW
  \item 72\,V absent $\rightarrow$ LED off $\rightarrow$ phototransistor off $\rightarrow$ pull-up holds GPIO HIGH
  \item $R_{\text{current\_limit}}$: $\sim$33\,k\textOmega, 2\,W. At 72\,V: $I \approx 2.2$\,mA. $P \approx 0.16$\,W.
\end{itemize}

\section{MCP4725 Throttle DAC Comparison}

Two MCP4725 12-bit I$^2$C DACs produce 0--5\,V analog signals for the motor controller throttle input.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{SYS (Level 2)} & \textbf{MTR (Level 1)} \\
\midrule
I2C address    & \iicaddr{60}          & \iicaddr{61} (A0 tied to VCC) \\
I2C SDA        & \gpio{15}            & PB7 \\
I2C SCL        & \gpio{16}            & PB6 \\
I2C bus        & I2C\_NUM\_0 (separate) & Separate I$^2$C bus \\
VCC            & 5\,V                  & 5\,V \\
VOUT range     & 0--5\,V              & 0--5\,V \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{MCP4725 Fast Write Command:}
\begin{lstlisting}
Byte 0: [0 0 PD1 PD0 D11 D10 D9 D8]  -- C2=C1=0 (Fast Write), PD1=PD0=0 (normal)

\end{lstlisting}
