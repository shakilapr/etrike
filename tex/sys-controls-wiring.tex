\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries SYS ESP32-S3 $\rightarrow$ 12 V Relay Wiring}
\end{center}

Relay: 12~V, 8-channel, \textbf{active-LOW} (IN LOW = relay ON).
If your board is active-HIGH, skip the ULN and connect GPIO $\rightarrow$ IN direct.

\section*{1. Power}

\begin{verbatim}
+12 V (fused) -> Relay module VCC
GND common: Relay GND = ULN2803 GND = ESP32 GND = lamp minus
\end{verbatim}

\section*{2. Outputs: ESP32 $\rightarrow$ ULN2803A $\rightarrow$ Relay IN}

\begin{tabular}{l l l l}
\toprule
\textbf{ESP32} & \textbf{ULN2803} & \textbf{Relay} & \textbf{Function}\\
\midrule
GPIO48 & IN1 $\rightarrow$ OUT1 & IN1 / K1 & AUTO indicator\\
GPIO39 & IN2 $\rightarrow$ OUT2 & IN2 / K2 & MANUAL indicator\\
GPIO17 & IN3 $\rightarrow$ OUT3 & IN3 / K3 & READY indicator\\
GPIO20 & IN4 $\rightarrow$ OUT4 & IN4 / K4 & \textit{(Unused - USB conflict)}\\
GPIO14 & IN5 $\rightarrow$ OUT5 & IN5 / K5 & BYPASS amber indicator\\
GPIO18 & IN6 $\rightarrow$ OUT6 & IN6 / K6 & \textbf{ESTOP red indicator}\\
GPIO19 & IN7 $\rightarrow$ OUT7 & IN7 / K7 & \textit{(Unused - USB conflict)}\\
GPIO21 & IN8 $\rightarrow$ OUT8 & IN8 / K8 & Brake lamp\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\vspace{1em}
\textbf{Special Note for Unused Pins (USB Conflict):} Relays K4 (GPIO20) and K7 (GPIO19) are connected to the ESP32-S3's Native USB data lines (D+ and D-). The side signal lamps and old ESTOP routing have been removed from the firmware. \textbf{Do not connect any side lamps or loads to Relay K4 or K7 while using USB!} If a USB cable is connected, the USB data signals will rapidly toggle these relays.

\begin{verbatim}
ESP32 GPIO -> ULN INx
ULN OUTx -> Relay INx
ULN COM: not used
GPIO40: not used - leave unconnected
\end{verbatim}

\section*{3. Lamps (relay contacts)}

\begin{verbatim}
+12 V (fused) -> Relay COM
Relay NO -> Lamp +
Lamp - -> GND
\end{verbatim}

\section*{4. Switches (each wire to GND)}

\begin{tabular}{l l l}
\toprule
\textbf{Function} & \textbf{GPIO} & \textbf{Switch}\\
\midrule
Brake lever & GPIO2  & Momentary NO\\
START       & GPIO41 & Momentary NO\\
MODE        & GPIO11 & Momentary NO\\
Bypass      & GPIO42 & Latching, closed at power-up = bypass ON\\
Left        & GPIO9  & (not wired)\\
Right       & GPIO6  & (not wired)\\
Headlight   & GPIO7  & (not wired)\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\section*{5. ESTOP switch}

\begin{verbatim}
GPIO1 -- ESTOP NC contact -- GND
Released (contact closed) = normal
Pressed (contact open) or broken wire = ESTOP
\end{verbatim}

\textbf{No resistors needed anywhere.} Every input uses the firmware internal
pull-up. GPIO1 must not be wired to 3.3\,V (older docs describe that scheme) --
against this firmware it reads as permanent ESTOP. The relay module contains its
own drivers.

\vspace{1em}
{\large\bfseries Never connect +12 V to any ESP32 GPIO.}
